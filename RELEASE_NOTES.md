# Bar Drink Props v1.0

First stable release of **Bar Drink Props** for GTA San Andreas Classic.

Ambient bar customers can perform their normal Rockstar drinking animations while visibly holding nothing. This mod restores a vanilla handheld bottle during the receive/drink sequence.

### Highlights

- Bottle appears during `Barcustom_get`, before the standing drink loop begins.
- Uses vanilla `CJ_BEAR_BOTTLE` (model 1484).
- CLEO+ render-object attachment keeps the prop on the right hand without altering ped AI/tasks.
- Supports both male and female standing drink loops.
- Automatic cleanup when drinking ends, the ped dies, or the ped disappears.
- Lightweight one-ped-per-tick scanning.

### Requirements

GTA San Andreas Classic, CLEO Redux, and CLEO+.
